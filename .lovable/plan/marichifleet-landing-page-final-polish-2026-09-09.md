# MarichiFleet landing page final polish

## Direction
Use the selected Industrial Precision direction with signal red, Space Grotesk/DM Sans, asymmetric editorial composition, hard-edged surfaces, disciplined whitespace, and restrained motion. Preserve every ERP, driver, portal, authentication, and admin route.

## Landing-page edit
- Reduce the page to seven purposeful scenes: Hero, Problem, Control, Live Movement, Delivery/POD, Billing/Profitability, Final CTA.
- Remove the boot sequence, custom cursor, floating clutter, generic statistics, duplicated lifecycle sections, repeated cards, and redundant calls to action.
- Keep the hero focused on MarichiFleet, the two-line promise, short support copy, two actions, and the existing 3D truck.
- Consolidate POD, invoice, payment, and profit into one continuous visual sequence.
- Keep the control tower focused on a map, selected vehicle, trip status, one alert, and timeline.

## Vehicle and motion
- Establish and verify the GLB’s true forward axis, then orient the truck once at model level.
- Make travel, lane movement, wheel rotation, suspension, and camera direction agree throughout the hero scroll.
- Replace abrupt camera changes with smoothly interpolated three-quarter, side, and overhead shots.
- Limit each scene to one dominant motion and one supporting reveal; remove particles and looping decorative pulses.
- Retain reduced-motion and mobile fallbacks with lower rendering cost and shorter scroll choreography.

## Theme
- Add a compact Dark / Light / System control to the public navigation and persist the choice.
- Remove the forced-dark landing override so the public page follows its own theme selection.
- Add dedicated landing tokens for cinematic graphite dark mode and warm-paper editorial light mode.
- Adapt the 3D background, fog, body finish, key light, rim light, road, and shadows smoothly between themes.

## Navigation and conversion
- Keep only Platform, Solutions, Control Tower, Login, Get Started, and the theme control.
- Retain a compact mobile menu with accessible theme selection.
- End with “Every mile. Under control.”, Enter MarichiFleet, Book a Demo, brand, and a minimal footer.

## Verification
- Check desktop and mobile in Dark, Light, and System modes.
- Capture hero and key scroll frames to confirm truck direction, grounding, camera continuity, readable themes, no overlaps, and no horizontal overflow.
- Verify navigation, both primary actions, reduced motion, WebGL loading, console/network health, and unchanged application routes.
